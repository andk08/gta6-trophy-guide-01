Roadmap to the platinum ~200 hours · 1 playthrough · difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx)

Stage 1

Play the story 60–80 hours

Chapters 1–5 with Jason and Lucia. Take optional missions as they appear, scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with binoculars as you go — the 100% checklist counts progress made during the story.

Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. There is no mission replay, and cheats disable trophies and saving.

Stage 2

100% completion ~100 hours

After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist: any 70 of the 80 challenges, plus every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest. The thresholds are lenient — about 60% of true completion.

This stage earns the gold A Long Road and most of the silver and bronze along the way.

Stage 3

Cleanup ~10 hours

Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, the character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) and whatever miscellaneous trophies are left. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops with the last one.

Every quest giver tied to a trophy is still available after the story in free-roam.

Watch out — 1 possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia suggests meditating on the balcony at Jason’s house. Do it before Chapter 1 ends — it disappears after that, and it is unconfirmed whether it counts toward the trophy. Open its guide
